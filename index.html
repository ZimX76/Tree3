How tree(3) works

Build a new colored tree every round, starting from the fixed stump. Round 1 uses 1 node, Round 2 uses 2 nodes, Round 3 uses 3 nodes, and so on. Your goal is to make a tree that does not contain any tree you've already made.

When checking for a match, previous trees can be rotated or shifted around their connections, but their colors and connections must stay the same. If an earlier tree can fit inside your new tree, you lose. Your score is the total number of nodes you've successfully placed.

Choose a color and click a visible connection point. New nodes snap into place and automatically form the connector chain.
